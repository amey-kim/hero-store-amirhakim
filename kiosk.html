HOW IS YOUR MISSION COST CALCULATED?

  • Destination: RM5.00 for deliveries within Perlis, RM10.00 for other Peninsular Malaysia states, or RM18.00 for Sabah, Sarawak, and Labuan.

  • Delivery Speed: Standard delivery has no surcharge. Express delivery costs an additional RM8.00.
